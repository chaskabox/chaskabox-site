## Why these writing practices?

1. **Accuracy and helpfulness**: Google's [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [Generative AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) discourage scaled, unverified generic content.
2. **Metadata**: Google's [Control your snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet) supports accurate product-specific meta descriptions; Google may display a different snippet.
3. **Rich results**: Google's [Merchant listing structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) recommends accurate server-rendered `Product` markup matching the customer-facing product details, price and availability.
4. **UX**: [Baymard Product Page UX](https://baymard.com/research-articles/collections/product-page) and [Shopify product page guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/product-page) motivate scan-friendly benefits, clear product specifications and mobile readability.

## Editorial review gate

- Compare exact product name, flavour, original imagery and `pack` field with sealed package. External websites may show different editions, unit counts or formulas.
- Confirm every unsupported ingredient, allergen, nutrition, expiry, claim or certified status against the original label. Do **not** infer from brand-wide marketing text.
- Review product-facing prose separately from `source_note`: source notes are for staff preview only and must never appear on public product pages.
- Keep SEO titles unique and short (this project's limit is 60 characters). Keep descriptions informative and focused (this project's admin validation allows up to 155 characters).
- Only use the authenticated admin's explicit Save action to publish; preserve prices, inventory, payment methods, WhatsApp, and existing product media.
- These 52 drafts leave **210 of 262 total product records** without a manufacturer/retailer-source-backed editorial draft; the current priority is visible products. The product-page display template applies to all products once deployed, independent of editorial draft status.
