DELIVERY INFORMATION

Shipping across Pakistan.

ChaskaBox currently delivers across Pakistan. The estimated delivery window shown by the store is 4–7 days.

Delivery charges

  • Cash on Delivery: Rs. 300 delivery charge. For new COD orders, Rs. 500 must be paid via JazzCash or bank transfer and verified before dispatch. It is deducted from the total; the rest is paid on delivery.
  • Prepaid (JazzCash or Bank Transfer): Orders of Rs. 5,000 or more qualify for free delivery; below that threshold the delivery charge is Rs. 300.

Availability and substitutions

Because products are sourced after an order is received, an item can occasionally become unavailable. ChaskaBox will contact the customer before removing or substituting an unavailable item. We do not intentionally make silent substitutions.

Order processing

ChaskaBox follows a sourced-after-order model, so products are sourced and packed after an order is received. Delivery estimates are not a guaranteed courier deadline and can be affected by destination, courier operations, public holidays or other disruptions.

Address changes

If you notice an address or phone-number mistake after ordering, contact ChaskaBox on WhatsApp as soon as possible and include your order number. Changes may not be possible once a parcel has been dispatched.
