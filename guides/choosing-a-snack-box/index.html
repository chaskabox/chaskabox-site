How to compare snack packs | ChaskaBox

A product name can cover several pack sizes. Check the quantity shown before comparing prices or ordering for a group.

Start with the quantity

“Pack of 12” is the listed retail pack quantity. It does not automatically mean twelve servings. Weights and portions depend on the original manufacturer label.

Compare matching packs

A pack of 48 and a pack of 12 are different purchases. Compare the current price and exact quantity together. For mixed Chaska Boxes, review the included retail packs. Displayed savings compare current individual prices with the box price.

Choose for the occasion

Explore bachpan picks, movie night or an office selection. Change quantities or remove packs in your bag.

Check labels and delivery

Ask ChaskaBox to confirm ingredients, allergens, dietary needs or best-before from the original label before ordering. Read the delivery policy; final charges appear at checkout.
