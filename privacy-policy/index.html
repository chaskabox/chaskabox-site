PRIVACY

How your information is used.

ChaskaBox uses only the information needed to provide shopping, account, delivery, support and store-security features.

Information you may provide

  • Name, phone number, delivery address and city during checkout.
  • Email/account information if you create or use an account.
  • Transaction/reference information you submit for supported prepaid methods. ChaskaBox does not ask for card PINs, OTPs or CVVs through the website.
  • Messages, reviews or evidence you choose to send to support.

How the site uses it

Order and account data is used to create and fulfil orders, provide order history/tracking, verify supported payments, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. ChaskaBox uses Supabase for database/authentication services and Cloudflare for website delivery, security, rate limiting and optional free AI features.

WhatsApp and email notifications

When configured, transactional order notifications may be sent through the self-hosted WAHA WhatsApp integration and optional Resend email service. These channels receive only information required for the notification.

Free AI features

ChaskaBox may use Cloudflare Workers AI for product search, customer FAQ/shopping assistance and admin drafting/summaries. AI is not allowed to authorize payments, calculate authoritative order totals, change staff permissions, or deploy the store. Customer-assistant requests are designed to block common sensitive identifiers before AI processing; customers should never submit passwords, OTPs, card details or private banking credentials to the assistant.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics may be used for privacy-focused traffic/performance measurement. Microsoft Clarity may be enabled on non-sensitive public pages for usability diagnostics; sensitive areas such as checkout, account, tracking and admin are excluded by the site configuration.

Browser storage

The website uses browser storage for cart contents, wishlist, recently viewed products and preferences. Full checkout name, phone and delivery address are not intended to be retained in local storage after a successful order.

Security and retention

Administrative access is role-restricted and sensitive credentials remain server-side. ChaskaBox keeps order/account information for legitimate operational, security and record-keeping needs and removes or limits access when it is no longer reasonably required.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact Chaskabox.mzg@gmail.com or WhatsApp 0332-0005381.
